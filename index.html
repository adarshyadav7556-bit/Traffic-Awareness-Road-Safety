About Traffic Awareness

Traffic awareness encourages responsible behavior on roads and helps people understand the importance of safe travel.

Wearing a helmet, using a seat belt, following signals and avoiding distractions are simple habits that matter.

  • Wear a properly fitted helmet on two-wheelers.
  • Use seat belts in cars and other vehicles.
  • Follow traffic signals and road signs.
  • Never drive while distracted.

Essential Safety Rules

Small actions can make a big difference. Follow these basic road-safety habits every day.

Wear Helmet

Always wear a properly fitted, approved helmet while riding a two-wheeler.

Use Seat Belt

Fasten your seat belt before the vehicle starts moving.

Follow Signals

Respect traffic lights, road signs and lane markings.

No Distraction

Keep your attention on the road and avoid using a phone while driving.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common traffic-safety questions.

Why is traffic awareness important?

It encourages responsible road behavior and helps people understand basic safety practices.

Why should I wear a helmet?

A properly fitted helmet is an important protective measure for two-wheeler riders.

Why is a seat belt important?

Seat belts are designed to help keep vehicle occupants properly restrained during a crash.

Can I use my phone while driving?

Avoid phone use and other distractions while driving so your attention remains on the road.
